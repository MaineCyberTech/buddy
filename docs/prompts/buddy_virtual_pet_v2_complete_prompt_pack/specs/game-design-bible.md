# Game Design Bible

## Core Fantasy
You hatch a tiny digital Buddy who lives inside a nostalgic LCD device. Your care, play, training, and adventures shape who they become.

## Design Pillars
1. Immediate delight: hatch first, signup later.
2. Deterministic uniqueness: your Buddy feels personally assigned.
3. Care without punishment: offline decay is gentle.
4. Collection depth: species, shiny, hats, decor, trinkets, journals.
5. Mobile-native sessions: playable one-handed.
6. Original LCD identity: ASCII sprites and segmented UI are the aesthetic center.

## Tone
Warm, lightly funny, cozy, curious, never mean-spirited.

## Progression Philosophy
Care improves outcomes, but players should not feel forced to babysit constantly. Bad outcomes should be recoverable.
