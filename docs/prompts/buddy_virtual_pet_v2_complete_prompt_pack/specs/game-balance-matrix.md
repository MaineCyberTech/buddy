# Game Balance Matrix

## Needs Decay Suggested Baseline
| Need | Active Decay | Offline Decay | Sleep Modifier |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Hunger | medium | reduced | reduced |
| Fun | medium | reduced | paused/reduced |
| Hygiene | slow | slow | slow |
| Energy | restored by rest | restored during sleep | positive |
| Health | only drops from poor care/sickness | capped | capped |

## Action Effects Suggested Baseline
| Action | Primary Gain | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feed | hunger | possible overfeed | favorite food gives bond |
| Play | fun/bond | energy | personality flavor |
| Wash | hygiene/mood | none | prevents sickness |
| Rest | energy | time | dream chance |
| Talk | bond/wants | none | personality text |
| Train | skill XP | energy | stat-scaled |
| Heal | health | medicine | validates inventory |

## Economy Rules
Avoid grind. Common food cheap, medicine moderate, rare decor expensive, shiny-related cosmetics rare but non-paywalled.
